The challenge

Propose a new use case — and design it

Your task is to propose a new, meaningful use case — a new feature, functionality extension, or user flow for one or more areas (Feed, Shots, AI Chat) — and present it as a well-thought-out concept with mockups.

⚠️
This is not a redesign. We're not looking for tweaks to the existing UI — button placement, colors, or wording.

Product areas

Where you can build

1Feed

The Feed works like a social network. The user's main goal is to see what others create and post, and what's currently trending.

What users do: like, comment, follow creators, post their own work, and get inspired by others.
Directions to exploreNew ways to discover content · social mechanics around AI posts · remixing and collaboration · creator profiles and collections · trends and challenges · personalized discovery.
2Shots

Trending templates users can generate for themselves or adapt. The user picks a photo or template, taps “edit,” describes what they want (e.g. “add a boat on the sea in the background”), and the system generates a new image.

What users do: quickly create content from ready-made trending templates, adapt them, and try new formats without writing a prompt from scratch.
Directions to exploreDaily challenges · limited-time templates · personalized daily prompts · seasonal or location-based drops · gamification (streaks, rewards) · sharing mechanics · unlockable styles · community voting.
3AI Chat

A chat where users generate photos from their own prompt. They can send references, upload their own photos and ask to edit specific details, and create or edit videos.

What users do: turn an idea into a finished image through conversation, refine results iteratively, make targeted edits, and create videos.
Directions to exploreCreating and editing images through conversation · turning a vague idea into a clear concept · personalization based on the user's style and past work · support for remixes and iterative edits. Focus on fundamental product ideas, not minor interface changes.
